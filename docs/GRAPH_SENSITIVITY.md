# Does the neighbour graph change the results?

## In short (for people)

The toys treat two block groups as neighbours when a triangulation of their centres links them. On average only 76% of those links are real shared borders; the rest jump across gaps or water. Rebuilding the neutral baselines on true Census borders changes the typical (median) number of Democratic seats in 7 of 44 multi-district states in at least one election, and moves the seat distribution by more than 20% (total variation) in 3. The solver's own plans are not contiguous on true borders in 29 states (AL, AZ, CA, CO, FL, GA, IA, IL, KS, KY, MA, MD, ME, MI, MN, MO, NJ, NM, NV, NY, OH, PA, RI, SC, TN, TX, VA, WA, WI).

Use the Census-border baseline when exact contiguity matters (it is what courts and commissions count); the site graph stays the default in the toys, and both are selectable in the Neutral baseline panel.
