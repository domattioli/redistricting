# Districts that keep ZIP-code friendships together

## In short (for people)

For 44 states, a compact district map that keeps as many friendships between ZIP-code areas inside districts as it can (Meta's Social Connectedness Index at ZIP level, which, unlike the county level, sees neighbourhoods inside a county). Each map is placed among the 10,000 neutral maps of its state.

- Democratic seats over these states: 209 (2016 votes), 234 (2020), 195 (2024); the neutral medians add up to 213, 230 and 198.
- Maps outside the middle 90% of neutral maps in some election: AR, AZ, CA, RI, TX.
- Social ties follow migration, party and race; these maps are a lens, not a neutral proposal.

## For AI agents (detailed)

Generated by `python -m redistricting_addons.sci_zcta` (method in its docstring). Plans in `site/data/sci/districts/<ST>.zcta.json`. Ties kept = share of cross-ZCTA friendship weight (SCI x both ZCTA populations, own pairs left out) inside one district, distance-adjusted with each state's own fit; compactness cap = the county-weighted neutral median of cut edges.
