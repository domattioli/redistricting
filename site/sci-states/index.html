Fifty states, redrawn by who knows whom

The country regrouped by Meta's Social Connectedness Index: places whose people are Facebook friends end up in one state. Pick a version, colour the map, and click a state for its details. Cohesion = the share of a state's people's friendships that stay inside it. The index follows migration, party and race, so these maps are a lens, not a neutral proposal.

Click a state on the map or in the table.

Units are counties, with counties above 100,000 people split into census tracts. Votes are block-group estimates. Data: site/data/sci_states/; method: docs/SCI_STATES.md and docs/SCI_GROW.md in the repository.
